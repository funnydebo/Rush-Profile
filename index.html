FOUNDED AUGUST 2025

Built on Velocity, Strategy & Community.

In August 2025, RUSH Club launched with an uncompromising mission: dissolve the divide between traditional athletic competition and modern e-sports gaming at Yamamah University.

Within twelve months, Rush shattered club engagement benchmarks, ran over 30 physical and digital activations, formed major health partnerships, and united hundreds of university students across courts, pitches, and LAN arenas.

The Rush Trajectory

TWO ARENAS • ONE IDENTITY

Where Every Competitor Finds Their Stage.

Whether your pulse is fueled by grass, asphalt, or 240Hz monitors, RUSH Club is engineered to elevate your skill, teamwork, and championship drive.

Physical Sports Arena

Padel, Football, Volleyball & Fitness

From fierce padel matches and campus football leagues to athletic conditioning with professional fitness partners, we build collegiate grit and athletic excellence.

Electronic Sports Battleground

Competitive LANs, Console & PC Tournaments

High-stakes gaming brackets, stage commentary, and collegiate scrims. We scout, train, and celebrate Yamamah's top gaming contenders.
